About Freeverse

A living city, a dynamic economy and freedom to choose your role

Freeverse is a life and economy simulation game where players can take on several roles, from education and employment to business ownership, production, trade, public services and social participation.

The game is built around modular systems and a time-driven economy. The goal is to create a world where choices are meaningful and affect resources, opportunities, reputation and future paths.

Core features

Living economy

Income, expenses, loans, insurance, property and employment are connected through interacting systems.

Multiple career paths

Study, train, find a job, advance in a profession or build an independent business.

Choice and consequence

Economic and social decisions affect reputation, opportunity, access and quality of life.

Ongoing development

New systems are added gradually after implementation, integration and real testing.
