Support

Freeverse Support

For installation problems, crashes, save issues, feedback or accessibility concerns, contact the development team by email.

playfreeverse@gmail.com

Include these details

Please include the game version, device model, Android version, a clear description of the issue and the steps that caused it. Screenshots or a short video are helpful when available.

Response time

Support is currently managed by an independent development team. Replies may take several business days, especially during testing and release periods.

Store and account issues

For download, payment or store-account problems controlled by the distribution platform, contact that store's customer support directly.
