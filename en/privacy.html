Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Current release status

The first public version of Freeverse is planned as a free game without in-app purchases or advertising. This policy will be updated before any data collection, analytics, advertising, account or online service is enabled.

Information collected

The current planned build does not intentionally collect personal information through the game. Technical information may still be generated by the operating system, device manufacturer or app store outside PlayFreeverse control.

Saved game data

Local game progress may be stored on the user's device. Removing the game or clearing application data may delete local saves unless a future backup or cloud feature is introduced.

Children

Freeverse is not specifically directed at children. The applicable age rating will be determined through the relevant store and content-rating process.

Future services

If analytics, advertising, cloud saves, accounts or other third-party services are introduced, this page and the store data disclosures will be updated before release.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to playfreeverse@gmail.com.
