About

100 levels across 10 visual realms

ChainTrace is a level-based puzzle game where every move matters. Correct choices build a green path, while mistakes reveal a red broken chain. The campaign includes 100 progressive levels, 10 visual realms, Static and Dynamic modes, and star-based progression.

Core experience

Logic, patterns and tactical power-ups

Mirror, Freeze, Remove and Shield power-ups support puzzle solving. The game also includes Persian and English interfaces, separate music and SFX volume controls, and calm music for both menus and gameplay.
