About

100 levels across 10 visual realms

ChainTrace is a level-based puzzle game where every move matters. Correct choices build a green path, while mistakes reveal a red broken chain. The collection now includes Dynamic and Static ChainTrace, Full Cube Minesweeper, and Cube Surface Minesweeper, with stage selection and star-based progression.

Core experience

Logic, patterns and tactical power-ups

Mirror, Freeze, Remove and Shield power-ups support puzzle solving. The game also includes Persian and English interfaces, separate music and SFX volume controls, and calm music for both menus and gameplay.

New Cube Minesweeper Modes

Two more ways to play

Full Cube Minesweeper

Explore cells in a three-dimensional cube, reveal safe cells, flag mines, and rotate the puzzle to inspect different angles.

Cube Surface Minesweeper

Focus on the outer surfaces of the cube, with Easy, Normal and Hard difficulties, limited hints, and stage-star progression.

Layouts and features may evolve as development continues.
